# Financial Apps: "Dö IT Q", a personal financial planner inside BNI Mobile Banking

**Result:** converting just 10% of young Taplus customers who do not use mobile banking would unlock **Rp 578.84 bn in AUM** and Rp 8.68 bn in fee-based income.

> BNI Innovation League, Innovation Hub Semarang. Role: Innovator. Source for volumes: BNI DMA Division. Conversion, balance and fee assumptions as stated in the competition proposal.

## Context
- Gen Z and Millennials are **53.81%** of Indonesia's population (Gen Z 27.94%, Millennials 25.87%; 2020 Population Census, BPS).
- Everyday banking is moving from branches and cash to mobile. BNI Mobile Banking was rated the best-performing mobile banking app in 2020 to 2021 (CNBC).

## Problem
- **Customer:** spending is impulsive and cash flow is untracked; goals like travel, gadgets or education have no savings plan; separate budgeting apps add friction.
- **BNI:** 5,788,393 young Taplus accounts do not use mobile banking; low engagement means idle balances and weak cross-sell; no in-app feature drives savings or investment products.

**Problem statement:** how might BNI help 20 to 35 year olds plan their money inside mobile banking, while growing engagement and funds?

## Data: primary survey (March 2020, n = 219, age 20 to 35)
| Finding | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Have a bank account | 99% |
| Know their monthly spending | 76% |
| Do not record spending | 52% |
| Use mobile banking | 58% |
| Want a financial planner in mobile banking | 60% |

Awareness without habit (76% know their spending, 52% never record it). Demand sits in BNI's channel: 60% want the planner inside mobile banking, not a separate app.

## Solution: every feature answers one research finding
| Feature | What it does | Answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction highlights | Auto summary and detail of monthly cash flow | 52% do not record spending |
| Goals and planning | Wishlist, target, deadline and saving plan | No plan for personal goals |
| Product recommendations | Taplus, Tapenas, deposits or investments | Drives cross-sell for BNI |
| Progress report and sharing | Email updates; achievements shared to Instagram and WhatsApp | Fits a visual, social segment |

## Impact
| | Accounts | AUM | Fee-based income |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing customers aged 20 to 35 with Taplus but no mobile banking | 5,788,393 | | |
| 10% conversion | 578,839 activated | Rp 578.84 bn (Rp 1 mn average balance) | Rp 8.68 bn (Rp 15k per account) |
| Upside from new accounts (based on 862,864 average new accounts, last 3 months) | 949,150 | Rp 949.15 bn | Rp 14.24 bn |

**For customers:** visible cash flow, a funded plan for each goal, and one app for banking and planning.

## Limits
Survey sample is small (n = 219) and dates from March 2020; conversion, balance and fee figures are proposal assumptions, not results.
