# e-Customer Service ("D'Serve"): e-channel guidance, e-forms and queue reservation in BNI Mobile Banking

**Result:** a feature set that resolves customer needs before they reach the branch and makes unavoidable visits queue-free. Reservation alone only matches the competitor; the edge is redirecting customers to e-channels and pre-filling forms before they arrive.

> Binnova Championship 2022. Role: Innovator.

## Context
- Mobile banking, ATMs and Agen46 cover most everyday banking transactions, and digital is a strategic pillar of BNI's consumer banking strategy for 2021 to 2025.
- But awareness is low: customers who do not know these services still come to the branch and wait for frontline staff.
- The chain to break: unaware of e-services, visits the branch, long queue at the counter.

## Problem
| Who | Pain |
| --- | --- |
| Customers | Hours lost waiting for services that could often be done digitally |
| Frontline staff | Heavy walk-in workload with only a few counters actively serving |
| BNI | Low branch ratings on Google that any prospective customer can see |

**Problem statement:** how might BNI resolve customer needs before they reach the branch, and make unavoidable visits queue-free?

## Data
Google Maps ratings of four Central Java branches range from **2.9 to 4.4** out of 5 (81 to 149 reviews per branch). Recurring themes: long waiting times (morning until midday or afternoon), many customer service desks but only a few staffed, and needs that could have been handled digitally.

**Benchmark: BCA already offers in-app branch reservation** (with estimated service time of 10 to 25 minutes per transaction).

| Feature | BCA | D'Serve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer service reservation | Yes | Yes |
| Estimated service time | Yes | not shown |
| Pre-filled e-form | not visible | Yes |
| Nearest branch and e-channel | not visible | Yes |
| Complaint handling via chat | not visible | Yes |

"Not visible" means not seen on the benchmarked screen.

## Solution: solve it digitally first, reserve the branch only if needed
1. **Nearest e-channel and branch.** Shows ATMs, Agen46 and branches around the customer.
2. **Online chat and contact center.** Routes complaints that do not need a face-to-face visit.
3. **E-form.** Customer data is pre-filled, so there is no re-entry at the counter.
4. **Queue reservation.** Teller or customer service slot booked at the chosen branch.

Sponsor units covered e-channel data, service management and mobile-banking and frontline system integration.

## Impact
- **BNI:** shorter queues and lighter frontline workload; customer data captured without re-entry and paperless e-forms; stronger digital awareness, brand and online ratings.
- **Customers:** complaints resolved without visiting a branch; a reservation number when a visit is unavoidable; knowing the nearest BNI branch and e-channel services.

**Suggested KPIs:** walk-in volume per branch, average waiting time, share of complaints closed via chat, and Google rating trend.
