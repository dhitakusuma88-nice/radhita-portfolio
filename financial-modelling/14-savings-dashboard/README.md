## 2. Movement (index, Nov 2021 = 100)
- **December: year-end inflow, +5.8% vs November.** Government and payroll accounts drive cash-in.
- **January: cash-out, -4.0% vs December.** Year-start outflows from business and program accounts.
- **April: spring peak, +3.6% vs March.** The highest point of the year.
- **October: below target, -1.9% vs September.** Outflows exceed inflows in the month.

## 4. Accounts
Balances: entrepreneur and merchant are up. Number of accounts: payroll is up, merchant slipped. Each segment is tracked as an index (Jan 2021 = 100) for balances and for account counts.
