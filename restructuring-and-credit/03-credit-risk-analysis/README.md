# Credit risk analysis: Bank XYZ, 2021 to 2025

**Result:** credit quality is under control, and efficiency is the real risk. Provisions and capital are strong, but costs outran income and returns collapsed.

> Bank XYZ (name withheld). Source: 2025 annual report, five years of data, Excel model. IDR billion unless stated.

## Step 1: loans and provisions
| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Gross loans, 2021 to 2025 | +63% (IDR 39.2 tn to 64.0 tn) |
| Loan growth in 2025 alone | +20.3% (after only 2.3% in 2024) |
| Impairment allowance (CKPN) as share of gross loans | 4.0% to 4.5% every year |

## Step 2: the regulator's ratio set
| Ratio | 2025 | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital adequacy | 25.4% | Three times the 8% minimum |
| Gross NPL | 2.19% | Well under the 5% ceiling |
| Operating cost to income (BOPO) | 93.9% | Up from 78.0% in 2023 |

## Step 3: derived metrics the report does not show directly
| Metric | 2025 | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NPL coverage (CKPN / estimated NPL) | 187% | Provisions exceed estimated NPL |
| Cost of risk proxy (new provisions / gross loans) | 0.45% | Low |
| BOPO vs 90% efficiency threshold | +3.9 pp | Above threshold |
| ROA vs 1.5% healthy benchmark | -0.99 pp | Below benchmark |

## Step 4 and 5: what the charts show
- **Provisioning kept pace:** CKPN stayed at 4.0% to 4.5% of loans while loans grew 63%.
- **Net NPL crept up:** gross NPL eased to 2.19%, but net NPL rose to about 1.0%, so provisions cover less of the problem loans.
- **Costs outran income:** BOPO 78% to 85% to 94% across 2023 to 2025.
- **Returns collapsed:** ROA 0.51% and ROE 2.98% in 2025.
- **Margin shrank every year:** NIM fell from 5.19% to 3.72%.
- **Funding got pricier:** CASA slipped to 42.6%; LDR 77.5%, just under the 78% to 92% range.

## Verdict
Credit is under control; efficiency is the real risk.

**What I would monitor next**
- NPL by origination year: the 2025 loan surge can flatter the ratio before new loans season
- Restructured and special-mention loans: tomorrow's NPL
- Stage 2 share and its coverage (PSAK 71)
- Cost of risk vs NIM: is growth paying for its risk?
- Borrower and sector concentration
